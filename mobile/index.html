Tor

Checking…

On, Tor tabs are ready straight away. Off, the app never contacts the Tor network until you open a Tor tab.

Tor tabs load .onion and https:// sites through Tor, and nothing they fetch is cached or written down. Signal, mail and telephone links still leave the app.

Background work

When enabled, Android keeps NCC running with an ongoing notification while you use another app. The system can still pause or stop work to save battery. Force-stopping NCC ends background work.

    Vault

    Never save

    The vault is one NIP-78 event, encrypted to your default identity and synced through its relays. Other identities can sign in independently; their prior vaults are merged here once they are available. Desktop and Android share the vault when they use the same default identity.

    Sync

    Keep bookmarks, open tabs and history in step with your other devices. Everything is encrypted to your own Nostr key before it leaves this phone and stored on your relays, so only you can read it. Private and Tor tabs are never shared. Passwords sync separately, in their own vault.
