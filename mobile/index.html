Never save

The vault is one NIP-78 event, encrypted to your own key on this device and synced through your relays. The desktop build reads the same event, so the same logins appear here when both use the same npub.

Sync

Keep bookmarks, open tabs and history in step with your other devices. Everything is encrypted to your own Nostr key before it leaves this phone and stored on your relays, so only you can read it. Private and Tor tabs are never shared. Passwords sync separately, in their own vault.

Sync is encrypted to your identity. Create or unlock one under Your identity first.
